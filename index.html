About

I am a PhD researcher in Brain, Mind & Computer Science at the University of Padua, funded by and working with Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK). I build machine learning models for clinical data, with a focus on predicting exacerbations of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) and on how clinicians come to trust AI-generated outputs.

Before the PhD I worked at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice on deep learning for EEG-based epilepsy prediction and on large language models for cognitive analysis, after an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence (University of Padua, 2023).
