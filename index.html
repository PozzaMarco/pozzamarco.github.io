About

I am a PhD candidate in Brain, Mind & Computer Science at the University of Padua, funded by and working with Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK). I build machine learning models for clinical data, with a focus on multimodal prediction of hospital readmission in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) and on how clinicians come to trust AI-generated outputs.

Before the PhD I collaborated on EEG-based seizure prediction research at the University of Padua and built RAG systems for knowledge management at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, after an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence (University of Padua, 2023).

Projects

What I am working on, and what it involves.

2 papers in preparation

Multimodal prediction of COPD readmission

Fusing structured EHR, ECG, chest X-rays and clinical notes from MIMIC-IV to predict 30-day hospital readmission in COPD patients, using frozen pre-trained encoders (ECG-JEPA, BiomedCLIP, Bio_ClinicalBERT) and gated fusion. The focus is rigorous evaluation: strict temporal leakage control, calibration, informative missingness and statistical power.

  • Multimodal deep learning
  • MIMIC-IV
  • Clinical prediction
